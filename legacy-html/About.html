Rhinoplasty

Overview

Rhinoplasty is a highly personalized procedure designed to harmonize your facial features by reshaping the nose. Dr. Gamal focuses on preserving breathing function while achieving aesthetically pleasing, natural-looking results.

Recovery

Most patients return to light activities within a week. Initial swelling subsides significantly after 2-3 weeks, with final refined results becoming apparent over the following months.

FAQ

  • How long does the surgery take? Typically 2-3 hours.
  • Will there be visible scars? Closed rhinoplasty incisions are hidden inside the nose.

Face & Neck Rejuvenation

Overview

Our face and neck procedures are tailored to reverse signs of aging by restoring volume and repositioning underlying tissues. The goal is a refreshed, rested appearance, not a "pulled" look.

Recovery

Downtime varies depending on the specific procedures performed, but typically ranges from 10 to 14 days for initial social recovery.

FAQ

  • What is a deep plane facelift? An advanced technique that lifts deeper tissues for longer-lasting, natural results.
  • Can this be combined with eyelid surgery? Yes, combination procedures are common and efficient.

Body Contouring

Overview

Body contouring encompasses procedures like liposuction and tummy tucks to remove stubborn fat and excess skin, creating a firmer, more sculpted physique.

Recovery

Recovery depends on the extent of the surgery. Most patients require 1-3 weeks off work and wear compression garments for several weeks to optimize results.

FAQ

  • Is this a weight loss procedure? No, body contouring is for refining shape after weight stability.
  • Are the results permanent? Fat cells removed are gone, but maintaining a stable weight is key.

Breast Procedures

Overview

Whether seeking augmentation, lift, or reduction, we offer tailored approaches to achieve breasts that beautifully complement your body's natural proportions.

Recovery

Most patients resume normal, non-strenuous activities within a week. Full release to vigorous exercise usually occurs around 6 weeks post-op.

FAQ

  • What types of implants do you use? We discuss silicone and saline options based on your goals.
  • Will a lift affect sensation? Temporary changes are common, but long-term issues are rare.
