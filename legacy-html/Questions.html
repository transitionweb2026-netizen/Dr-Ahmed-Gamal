COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, concise information about procedures, consultations, preparation, recovery, safety, and follow-up.

How do I know which procedure is right for me?

add remove
Your surgeon will evaluate your goals, medical history, and anatomy during a thorough consultation to recommend the most suitable approach tailored specifically for you.

What happens during the first consultation?

add remove
You will discuss your aesthetic goals, review your medical history, and explore potential procedures. The surgeon will outline risks, benefits, and costs to ensure you are fully informed.

How should I prepare for plastic surgery?

add remove
Preparation typically involves avoiding certain medications, quitting smoking, arranging for post-operative care, and following any specific dietary or lifestyle instructions provided by your surgeon.

What tests may be required before surgery?

add remove
Standard preoperative tests may include blood work, an ECG, and specific imaging studies depending on the procedure and your individual health profile to ensure you are fit for surgery.

Is plastic surgery safe?

add remove
When performed by a board-certified plastic surgeon in an accredited facility, plastic surgery is generally very safe. However, like all surgical procedures, it carries inherent risks that will be discussed in detail.

What type of anesthesia may be used?

add remove
Depending on the extent of the procedure, you may receive local anesthesia, intravenous sedation, or general anesthesia. Your surgeon will recommend the safest and most comfortable option.

How long does the procedure usually take?

add remove
Surgical duration varies widely based on the complexity and number of procedures being performed, ranging from under an hour for minor adjustments to several hours for extensive surgeries.

Will I need to stay in the hospital?

add remove
Many plastic surgery procedures are performed on an outpatient basis, meaning you can return home the same day. More extensive surgeries may require an overnight stay for observation.

How long is the recovery period?

add remove
Initial recovery usually takes one to two weeks, during which bruising and swelling subside. Full healing and final results can take several months, depending on the specific procedure.

When can I return to work?

add remove
Return to work depends on the physical demands of your job and the extent of the surgery. Most patients resume sedentary work within one to two weeks.

When will the final results appear?

add remove
While you will notice immediate changes, final results may take 6 to 12 months to fully emerge as residual swelling resolves and tissues settle into their new contours.

Will there be visible scars?

add remove
All surgeries involve some scarring. However, experienced surgeons strategically place incisions in natural skin folds or hidden areas to ensure scars remain as inconspicuous as possible.

How long do results usually last?

add remove
Many plastic surgery results are long-lasting or permanent. However, aging, lifestyle choices, and weight fluctuations can affect the longevity of your outcomes.

What should I avoid during recovery?

add remove
Patients are generally advised to avoid strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, sun exposure on scars, and smoking to promote optimal healing and minimize complications.

How many follow-up visits are needed?

add remove
Follow-up care usually involves an initial post-operative visit within a week, followed by periodic check-ups over the next several months to monitor your progress.

What are the possible risks and complications?

add remove
While rare, potential risks include infection, bleeding, unfavorable scarring, and anesthesia reactions. Adhering to your surgeon's pre- and post-operative instructions minimizes these risks.

Can multiple procedures be performed together?

add remove
Yes, combining procedures (like a "Mommy Makeover") is common and can reduce overall recovery time and costs, provided you are a suitable candidate for longer surgery.

How do I maintain my results?

add remove
Maintaining a stable weight, adopting a healthy lifestyle, protecting your skin from the sun, and following a good skincare regimen are key to preserving surgical outcomes.

What should I bring to my consultation?

add remove
Bring a list of your medical history, current medications, past surgeries, and a list of questions or concerns you wish to discuss with the surgeon.

How can I book an appointment?

add remove
You can easily book a consultation by contacting our clinic via phone, WhatsApp, or through the direct booking links provided on our website.
